# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a community where every learner and contributor can
participate without harassment — regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience
level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We interact in ways that make the space open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive
and healthy.

## Standards

Behaviour that strengthens the community:

- Empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respect for differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Graceful, constructive feedback — given and received.
- Owning mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Prioritising the collective good over individual convenience.

Behaviour that is not tolerated:

- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory remarks, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, emails, photos) without consent.
- Any other conduct a reasonable person would find unprofessional.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders clarify and enforce these standards and take fair corrective
action for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that breach this code, and will explain their
reasoning when they do.

## Scope

This code applies in all community spaces and when someone is officially
representing the community in public — e.g. via an official email address,
social media account, or as a designated representative at an event.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the community leaders at
**conduct@study-rpg.app** (placeholder — replace with the real address). Every
complaint is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Reporters' privacy
and security are respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Leaders follow these impact guidelines when deciding consequences:

### 1. Correction
**Impact**: inappropriate language or unprofessional behaviour.
**Consequence**: a private written warning explaining the issue and why it was
inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Impact**: a single violation or a series of actions.
**Consequence**: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No
unsolicited interaction with the people involved, including in community
spaces and external channels, for a specified period. Further violations may
lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban
**Impact**: a serious violation of community standards, including sustained
inappropriate behaviour.
**Consequence**: a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication.
No interaction with the people involved is permitted during the ban; violating
this may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban
**Impact**: a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward
individuals or groups.
**Consequence**: a permanent ban from all public interaction within the
community.
