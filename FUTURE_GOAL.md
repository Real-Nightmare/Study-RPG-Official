# Study RPG — Roadmap & Direction

> **Status**: 2026-08-16. The Study RPG transformation (10 PDF phases 0–9, the
> community track, Free-to-Win Integrity, Anti-OverStudy Wellbeing, and the
> Ocean data marketplace) is delivered — see `IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` for the
> live status. This document captures where the project is going next.

## North star

A learning platform that is **good for the learner first**: measurable
mastery, honest rewards, and a system that actively protects your rest and
focus. The RPG layer exists to make *real* study satisfying — never to
manufacture grind.

## Completed milestones

- **Phase 0–9** — platform foundation, production RAG, study tools, dashboard,
  RPG core, economy, events, community, hardening, wellbeing.
- **Free-to-Win Integrity (spec 014)** — exponential reward curve, anti-cheese
  guards, campfire metacognitive loop, zero pay-to-win.
- **Anti-OverStudy (spec 015)** — diminishing returns past a healthy daily
  optimum, rest cooldowns, night-rest guard, health bands.
- **Data Marketplace & Benchmark (spec 016)** — consent-gated aggregate
  publishing, Ocean Protocol Compute-to-Data on-chain publishing (Polygon
  mainnet), admin AI effectiveness benchmarking.

## What's next (candidate directions)

### Learner experience
- Deeper adaptive learning paths that re-plan from real mastery data.
- More original study-game content: new card families, bosses, and quest lines
  (all original — no third-party IP).
- Better rest-aware scheduling: surface *when to stop* as clearly as *what to
  study next*.

### Platform & scale
- Full test coverage of the marketplace and C2D publish paths against a live
  Ocean node (requires funded wallet + RPC).
- Multi-node Ocean deployment for the idle-capacity earnings mode.
- Observability: traces across AI + RAG + WebSocket flows.

### Data economy (sustainability)
- Expand consented, aggregate-only dataset types (study patterns, mistake
  taxonomy, subject pacing).
- Publish-on-Base support (one env change — addresses/RPC are already
  env-overridable).
- Public dashboards proving the benchmark methodology.

### Community
- Clean-room rewrite program (see `docs/audits/REWRITE_LEDGER.md`) — replace
  all upstream-derived files with original Study RPG work, after which the
  licence can be re-evaluated (gated on completion).
- Contributor onboarding: spec-first feature work via Spec Kit.

## How to influence the roadmap

Open a discussion or a feature request; the specs in `specs/` are the
authoritative planning surface for anything new.
